## 1. Visual bible (applies to every shot)

**Two worlds, one film**
| | Story World | Concept World |
|---|---|---|
| Look | 2D hand-drawn cel animation, painted textured backgrounds (matches M1) | Stylised cinematic 3D: soft matte materials, subtle reflections, volumetric light, shallow depth of field |
| Used for | People, real-life moments, emotions | Data, algorithms, machines, networks, abstract ideas |
| Palette | Muted teal + warm amber | Deep navy space with the channel colours as light sources: AI blue `#4F8CFF`, ML green `#22C55E`, DL amber `#F59E0B` |
| Camera | Gentle, human: slow push-ins, locked medium shots | Purposeful moves: dolly, orbit, crane, pull-back reveals |

**Rules**
- One idea per shot. Motion must explain something; nothing moves "just because".
- Concept World colours always mean the same thing: blue = AI, green = ML, amber = DL. Red only for errors and spam.
- No glitch effects, no lens-flare spam, no neon overload. Glow only on the thing that matters.
- No text inside generated video (it garbles). All labels are added in editing.
- No real people's faces generated by AI (Turing, Kasparov…). Real people appear only in real, credited photos; generated scenes show anonymous people from behind or in silhouette.
- Every clip ≤ 8 s. Longer beats are split into shots that cut on motion.

**Transition vocabulary** (each one has a meaning)
| Transition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Push into Mani's laptop screen → Concept World | "Let's look inside the idea" |
| Particles dissolve / re-form | One concept becomes another |
| Match cut on shape (circle → circle, hand → hand) | "Same idea, different world" |
| Painted brush wipe | Back to the Story World |
| Pull-back reveal | Show the bigger picture |

**Paste this STYLE line at the start of every FLOW prompt**
- Story World: `2D hand-drawn cel animation, premium animated short film, expressive ink linework, softly painted textured backgrounds, muted teal and warm amber palette, gentle cinematic lighting, subtle film grain, no text, no logos.`
- Concept World: `Stylised cinematic 3D animation, soft matte materials with subtle reflections, volumetric light rays, shallow depth of field, deep navy environment lit by blue, green and amber light, physically plausible motion with gentle motion blur, no text, no logos, no glitch effects.`

Camera performs a slow dolly forward into the brain, passing between glowing neural branches. Four distinct clusters ignite one after another, each a different rhythm of light.
